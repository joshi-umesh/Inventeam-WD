Meet Raseen Khan and the entrepreneurial journey behind Royal Industries.

Raseen Khan – A Serial Entrepreneur with a Vision for Greater Success

Raseen Khan is a seasoned entrepreneur with a diverse business journey built on experience, adaptability, strategic thinking, and an unwavering commitment to growth. His entrepreneurial story began with the family’s shoe sole manufacturing business, which he inherited from his father. Building on this strong foundation, he expanded his horizons, transforming traditional business experience into opportunities across multiple industries.

Recognising the immense potential of organised retail, Raseen ventured into the ladies’ footwear segment, establishing a retail network for ladies’ shoes and chappals across India through the modern trade format. His understanding of market dynamics, consumer preferences, product positioning, and retail expansion subsequently led him to take on a consulting role, helping textile and other merchandise manufacturers explore and establish their presence in organised retail.

His entrepreneurial journey then extended into international trade, particularly the import and export of agricultural commodities. With a focus on sourcing, quality, supply chain management, and building dependable business relationships, he broadened his expertise from domestic manufacturing and retail to the opportunities and complexities of global commerce.

Each venture has added a new dimension to his business acumen, strengthening his ability to identify opportunities, build business models, develop markets, and turn ideas into sustainable commercial ventures.

Today, through his new enterprise, Royal Industries, Raseen Khan is embarking on his next and perhaps most ambitious chapter yet. Drawing upon his rich cross-industry experience, entrepreneurial instincts, and proven ability to adapt and expand, he is setting out to create another success story—one with a bigger vision, greater scale, and aspirations that reach far beyond his previous achievements.

For Raseen, entrepreneurship is not merely about building businesses; it is about recognising possibilities, creating value, and continuously raising the bar. With Royal Industries, he is bringing together the lessons of the past and the possibilities of the future to build an enterprise poised for its next phase of growth and success.
